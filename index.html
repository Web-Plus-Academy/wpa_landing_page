About Us

  • To create a future where students train students in cutting-edge skills.

Our Directors

Meet Founder

Saran Velmurugan

Founder & CEO of Web Plus Academy and Webnexz, a visionary entrepreneur with a passion for building impactful ecosystems that bridge education, technology, and innovation.

As a young founder, Saran excels in startup strategy, business development, technology leadership, and team building. His focus lies in nurturing talent, creating sustainable business models, and transforming ideas into scalable solutions.

At Web Plus Academy, his mission is to empower students to become industry-ready by providing not only technical education but also entrepreneurship pathways. With Webnexz, he drives innovation through a startup incubator model, encouraging students and young professionals to turn their ideas into real ventures.

His leadership reflects a strong belief in “Don’t Seek Jobs, Create Them”, inspiring the next generation to think beyond employment and embrace entrepreneurship.

Meet Co-Founder

Rupa Sree

Co-Founder of Web Plus Academy, Rupa Sree brings in her expertise in strategic operations, organizational growth, and student engagement. She plays a vital role in aligning the academy’s mission with long-term sustainability and impact.

Her strengths lie in building communities, fostering collaboration, and ensuring that every initiative of the academy supports both academic excellence and entrepreneurial thinking.

Alongside operational leadership, she focuses on cultural development—creating a supportive ecosystem where students and young professionals feel motivated to innovate, experiment, and grow as leaders.

With her guidance, Web Plus Academy continues to be a place that inspires confidence, nurtures talent, and transforms potential into real-world success stories.
